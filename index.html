How I work

Enough process to learn, not perform.

  1. 01

    Frame the right problem

    Build shared context around people, constraints, and what a useful outcome needs to change.

  2. 02

    Make the invisible tangible

    Use flows, prototypes, and plain-language decisions to turn assumptions into something the team can examine.

  3. Test the important parts

    Bring real feedback into the work early, then iterate toward clarity, confidence, and feasibility.

  4. 04

    Carry it through

    Partner with engineering and product through delivery, documenting decisions and learning from what ships.
